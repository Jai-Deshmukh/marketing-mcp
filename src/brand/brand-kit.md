## Tone
- Warm, practical, direct.
- Talk like a helpful friend, not an ad.
- Short sentences. One idea per video.

## Do
- Show real-life moments: asking a friend, getting a recommendation, a task getting done.
- Make the circle (trusted people) the hero, Hyv the enabler.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds; CTA at the end.
- Captions on (most social video is watched muted).

## Don't
- No hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, best ever, guaranteed…).
- No fake claims, made-up stats or testimonials.
- Never depict real people's likeness or celebrities.
- Never show logos other than Hyv's.
